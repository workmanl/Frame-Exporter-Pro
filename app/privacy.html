Privacy policy

Frame Exporter Pro, published by Emrld Labs.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Summary

Frame Exporter Pro processes all videos and still frames locally on your iPhone or iPad. Emrld Labs does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage analytics, or media files. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. The developer does not collect any data from this app.

What we collect

None. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

  • Daily free export count and the date of the last export (stored in local app preferences)
  • Premium Unlock purchase status managed by Apple StoreKit
  • Frames you choose to save to your Photos library or Files

Permissions

  • Photo Library (Read): Used to import videos that you select from your Photos library.
  • Photo Library (Add): Used to save exported still frames to your Photos library.

You can change these permissions at any time in Settings.

Network use

The app makes no network connections for video processing and contains no URLSession networking code. Network access is used only by Apple StoreKit when purchasing or restoring Premium Unlock, or by iOS if you import a file from iCloud Drive.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Emrld Labs never receives or sees your payment card or billing details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
